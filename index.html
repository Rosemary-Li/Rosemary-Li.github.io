Research

A Triangulate-and-Audit Framework for Measuring Depression-Indicative Self-Disclosure in YouTube Comments

accepted

Rosemary Li, Nuo Chen, Shenghan Gao, Zhihan Jiang
WellComp @ UbiComp/ISWC 2026, Shanghai

Triangulated a lexical–syntactic rule, SBERT + HDBSCAN clustering, and two LLM annotators over 10,867 comments across 222 videos. Found strong inter-LLM agreement (κ = 0.87) and far higher disclosure in mental-health than music content (OR = 19.1).

Student Signal Copilot

Jun 2026 — 3rd place, NYC Tech Week

Rosemary Li, Nuo Chen, Qijing (Kimberly) Huang
The City Hacks The State, with CriticalAsset and AWS

Students are the first sensors in a building. A supervisor agent turns a messy one-sentence report — a hallway leak, a broken fixture, an unsafe stairwell — into an auditable signal → evidence → action → verification workflow, coordinating specialists for signal extraction, asset grounding, public-data enrichment, compliance review, confidence labelling, recommendation generation, and systemic pattern detection. Outputs are grounded in CriticalAsset building assets and NYC public data, humans keep control of real-world actions, and the agent trace is shown instead of a black-box answer. The workflow streams live over server-sent events and routes tasks across model tiers for cost and reliability; the loop closes by asking the student whether the issue was actually fixed.

multi-agent / civic infrastructure / human-in-the-loop / auditable traces
